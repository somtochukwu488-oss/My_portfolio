Event & Tech Community Campaign

GDG Onitsha — DevFest Onitsha 2026

Role: Volunteer Social Media Manager • Theme: Build, Secure, Scale

LinkedIn • X • Instagram • Facebook • TikTok

The Objective

Drive pre-event awareness, build excitement across regional tech communities, spotlight speakers and partners, and execute real-time and post-event digital coverage for DevFest Onitsha 2026 (held November 7, 2026, at Vegas Astoria Hotel & Suites, GRA Onitsha).

Strategy & Execution

  • Multi-Phase Campaign Rollout: Structured content across pre-event momentum (daily countdowns, venue directions, community shirt promos), campus outreach promotion (Igbariam outreach & Pre-DevFest Hangout), and post-event retention.
  • Partner & Speaker Positioning: Crafted tailored spotlight copy for sponsors and partners including Onitsha Business School (OBS), Entech, Litehost, Prolific Hub, Ajatek, SpyExpress, and Keke Link.
  • Live & Post-Event Storytelling: Captured stage sessions, red carpet moments, and candid "unspoken snapshots," turning them into photo carousels, speaker nugget series, and volunteer appreciation highlights.

Sample Post Copy

"Behind every packed hall and smooth panel at DevFest Onitsha 2026 were months of quiet planning, late-night copy edits, and a community ready to Build, Secure, and Scale. From our Igbariam campus outreach to the final stage session at Vegas Astoria Hotel, watching builders connect in real time made every post worth it."

Brand Launch & Co-Working Community

Ctrl Space (CTRL+SPACE)

Role: Community & Social Media Lead • Location: Onitsha, Anambra State

Instagram • X • LinkedIn

The Objective

Launch and position Ctrl Space as a modern tech and lifestyle co-working community in Onitsha, building an engaged local audience ahead of its inaugural physical meetup on September 19, 2026, at Executive Mall, NTA GRA.

Strategy & Execution

  • Brand Voice & Aesthetic: Established a clean, minimalist visual identity and conversational brand tone tailored to remote workers, developers, and creatives.
  • Community-First Content: Created teaser graphics, value-driven posts around remote work culture, and clear event CTAs to convert online followers into in-person attendees.
  • Active Engagement: Managed comment sections, DMs, and community announcements to foster a welcoming, beginner-friendly atmosphere.

Sample Post Copy

"Working from home hits differently when you have a room full of people building alongside you. Ctrl Space is officially opening its doors for our first community meetup at Executive Mall, NTA GRA. Bring your laptop, bring your ideas, and pull up a chair."

Outcome: Built the community’s digital presence from zero and converted online engagement into strong physical turnout at Executive Mall, NTA GRA.
E-Commerce & Product Showcase

Queen' Store

Role: Content Creator & Social Media Copywriter • Niche: Home & Kitchen Essentials

Instagram • TikTok • WhatsApp Business

The Objective

Elevate the visual presentation of Queen' Store’s product catalog and write persuasive, conversion-focused social media copy to drive direct customer inquiries and orders.

Strategy & Execution

  • Clean Visual Merchandising: Designed uncluttered, mature promotional layouts showcasing stoneware dinner sets, wooden food platters, and ceramic food warmers.
  • Benefit-Driven Copywriting: Wrote scannable captions highlighting durability, hosting aesthetics, and everyday functionality, paired with clear ordering prompts.
  • Mobile-First Formatting: Structured product highlights for quick scannability and strong two-second visual hooks.

Sample Product Caption

"Hosting hits different when your table looks the part. Our minimalist stoneware dinner sets and ceramic food warmers are built to keep meals hot and your dining setup effortless—whether it’s a quiet Sunday dinner or family guests. Send a DM to place your order today."

Outcome: Standardized the brand’s visual catalog for a cleaner storefront aesthetic and increased product clarity for direct DM inquiries.
